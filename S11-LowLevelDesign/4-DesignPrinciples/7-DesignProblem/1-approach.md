### Low Level Design (50 mintutes)
- Crammed up solutions don't work
- Should also be very structured
- Expectation here is **not** to come up with a working solution rather than engage in **meaningful dialogue**

### Stages
1. Gathering Requirements (5-10 minutes)
    - Focus time exploring all the diffrent angles
    - Assumption --> State it upfront 
        - Confirm from the interviewer before proceeding
    - Confirm from the interviewer that al the requirements are met
    - Functional 
    - Non Functional
        - Modular code
        - Scalable
        - Low Coupling
2. Identifying Major Entities of the system (5 mintutes)
   - Generally 10- 15 entities 
   - Correlate it with the requirements, whether everything is met or not

3. Detail Design of the System (30 minutes)
    - Go entity by entity
    - Identify the relationship between entitites
    - Identify the attributes of these entities
    - Identify the behaviour of these entitites

    - Where to start?
        - Top to Bottom Approach 
            - Start with the biggest entity and try figuringn out the related entities
        - Bottom to top approach 
            - Start with the smallest entity and then build around it

4. Diagram (5 minutes)
5. Follow up questions (10 minutes) 
    - Cross questions on approach
    - Extend the design to accomodate a new use case
    - To write code for specific behaviour or a specific flow
